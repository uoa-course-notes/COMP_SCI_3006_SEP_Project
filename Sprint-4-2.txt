+ Model-Independent techniques: model agnostic 
- SHAP values  
- Partial Dependence Graphs
- Permutation Importance 

+ Local explanations: shedding lights on a model's prediction on a particular input or instance of the data set. This is useful for debugging and error analysis (can also be used to pin-point areas that need improvement)
- LIME (Local Interpretable Model-Agnostic Evaluation)
- Anchors 



+ Global explanations: give a broad knowledge of the model's decision-making processes in relation to all inputs. 
- SHAP values 
- ICE (Individual Conditional Expectation) plots 
- Counterfactuals: preferred by domain experts. 

- Gradient-Based Appproach: preferred by Data Scientists or  ML engineers to evaluate the model's feature attribution.
